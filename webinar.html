The Method

A five step method for reclaiming capacity.

01

Map the Handoffs

Trace every workflow boundary between people, tools, and inboxes where ownership gets dropped. Build a visual map of every place work changes hands.

02

Time Cost the Work

Quantify hours per week, per role, per workflow. Turn feels heavy into a defensible number backed by data your leadership team can act on.

03

Score Feasibility

Rate each workflow on data quality, exception volume, and integration complexity to find the ones that are actually fixable in the near term.

04

Rank by ROI

Sort by hours saved per dollar invested. Stop optimizing the loud problems and fix the expensive ones first.

05

Sequence the Roadmap

Build a 90 day sequence so each fix funds and de risks the next one. No two year transformation projects. Just clear measurable steps.
