About Moduvo

Built by someone who got tired of manual work.

David Lazovski is the founder of Moduvo and an automation consultant operating at the intersection of AI, operations, and growth. He works with eCommerce brands, SaaS companies, and logistics operators across Central and Eastern Europe to help them stop doing manual work and start scaling.

His stack is n8n for workflow automation, Claude AI for intelligent reasoning, and Cursor for application development. He believes automation should be owned by the client not the agency, which is why every Moduvo project includes full team training so clients are never dependent on external support.

Based in the CEE region, he works with a network of specialist partners in Poland and across Europe.
